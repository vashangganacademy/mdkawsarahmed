KAWSAR AHMED — PROFESSIONAL ENGLISH LANGUAGE TRAINER WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html       Home
- about.html       About the trainer
- expertise.html   Areas of expertise
- teaching.html    Teaching methodology
- courses.html     Course / learning pathway overview
- resources.html  Learning resources
- contact.html     Contact and enquiry form
- styles.css       Shared responsive styling
- script.js        Mobile navigation, year and email-form behaviour

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Navigate using the top menu.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace the KA placeholder with your real profile photo (recommended file: assets/profile.jpg).
2. Replace your.email@example.com in contact.html and script.js with your real email.
3. Replace # social links in contact.html and footer with your actual profiles.
4. Add only verified education, teaching experience and certifications.
5. Confirm course names, schedules, fees and enrolment details before advertising them.
6. For a live website, upload the folder to a hosting service or connect it to your domain.

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript starter website. The contact form uses mailto and does not include a backend or database.
