About Kawsar Ahmed

My Story

Helping learners find their voice in English.

I am an aspiring English Language Trainer passionate about helping learners develop natural, confident and effective English communication skills.

My approach focuses on practical language use rather than memorisation. I believe learning English is about understanding, thinking and communicating naturally in real-life situations.

I aim to create a supportive and interactive learning environment where learners can overcome hesitation, improve pronunciation and develop fluency.
